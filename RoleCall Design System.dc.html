The RoleCall design system

01Foundations

Color

Ink on paper, plus tonal ramps for the two process inks. Cyan carries interaction; magenta is the rarer second voice; a muted press yellow and a sage green round out the pipeline. Body copy never runs in a raw ink — use the 700+ steps.

09Patterns

Dialogs

Modals float on an overlay-elevation card over a dimmed sheet. A form dialog for creating, a confirm dialog for destructive intent. Click a button to open one.

10Patterns

Pull-out drawer

A right-docked panel for quick tasks that shouldn't steal the whole page — adding an application, editing contacts, filtering the board. Slides in over a dimmed sheet; dismiss with the sheet, the ✕, or Cancel.
